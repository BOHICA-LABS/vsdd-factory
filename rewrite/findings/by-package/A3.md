Where it was found: rewrite/spec/platform.md items 9 and 33; rewrite/spec/topics/research-and-browser-tools.md; rewrite/spec/topics/tool-sets.md

The 127.0.0.1-through-WebFetch route is refuted and stays dropped. Record both the undotted-hostname refusal and the http-to-https upgrade in the browser-tools topic so the e2e walls expect them.
