## CL-R2 (minor; close-out of pass 2; open)

Files: commands/survey.md, tables/preconditions.toml, tables/routes.toml

Where it was found: state.md 2.16 vsdd survey row (line 1379); BLUEPRINT.md 4.11 (lines 656-660) and 11.4 N2 row (line 1733); pipeline.md 2.9.3 survey row (line 950); pipeline.md 2.5 plan precondition (line 383); pipeline.md 2.4 spec row (line 297) and line 336; conflicts with state.md 2.5 staleness row (line 644) and packaging.md line 1006; clearing rule at state.md 2.8 lines 803-805

Because of N2, an earlier spec approval stays current at the start of a later product cycle, and three rules cannot tell it apart from the cycle's own approval. (1) The spec survey's amendment mark is unscoped ('when the latest spec decision is approved'). A spec run started by hand or by mistake in the middle of a product cycle whose own spec is approved now unlocks the whole set with no change analysis, so plan has no stories to re-trace afterwards. (2) The plan precondition is met before spec runs in a later product cycle, so a plan started too early is not refused, contrary to 'that workflow's survey refuses'. (3) The route's spec row now does all the work of sending that cycle to spec, and it depends on the undefined phrase 'the specification is not converged'.

Proposed resolution: Use one predicate, 'the cycle's own spec decision' (one in cycles/<cycle>/decisions/). (1) The spec survey marks the amendment only when the cycle began with a brief and has no spec decision of its own; change state.md 2.16, pipeline.md 2.9.3 (drop the parenthesis) and blueprint 4.11 with its N2 row. (2) The spec row reads 'the brief is approved and the cycle has no spec decision of its own, or its latest one asked for revision'. (3) The plan precondition adds 'in a cycle that began with a brief, the approved spec decision is the cycle's own'. Also state that this survey mark is not 'opening an amendment' for the state.md 2.8 clearing rule.
