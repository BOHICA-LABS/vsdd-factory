Files: commands/survey.md, tables/sync-checks.toml, tables/team-delivery-checks.toml

Where it was found: rewrite/spec/tables/team-delivery-checks.toml (T-BRANCH, T-NOTICE); rewrite/spec/tables/sync-checks.toml (T-FRESH); rewrite/spec/commands/survey.md (Team use)

T-FRESH says the opening sync (60 seconds) and the claim (30 seconds) together keep the survey inside the Bash tool's default limit of two minutes. The survey makes more network calls than those two: in pr mode the fetch of the target branch (T-NOTICE), and for each story the clone holds whose branch was pushed a listing and a fetch (T-BRANCH). Each has the time limit of a network call, but nothing bounds them together, so on a slow remote a clone that holds several pushed stories passes the limit, and the survey is not a job. Nor does any row say what the survey does with a story whose branch could not be listed or fetched: the head rule only says that it is `unreachable` and never 'nothing arrived'.

Proposed resolution: Give those calls one stated budget and one outcome. A story whose branch could not be read inside it is not worked on in this run: its unit is returned blocked with the reason `environment`, the others go on, and the next survey measures again. State this in T-BRANCH and correct the sentence of T-FRESH. Test: a survey on a clone that holds five pushed stories, against a remote that delays every call, returns inside the limit and names the stories it could not read.
