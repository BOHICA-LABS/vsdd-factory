# Research report
The machine row for this kind - path, identifier, frontmatter keys, cap, writer and lifecycle - is its row in [[table:artifacts]].

There is no lessons register. A report of kind `retrospective` carries the cycle's proposals, each naming
the line it would add to `conventions.md` or `review-rules.md`; a person adopts one with
`/vsdd-factory:author conventions` or `author review-rules` ([[artifact:project-conventions]],
[[artifact:review-rules]]).

Collected feedback and analytics that feed research are not reports: they are written by `vsdd discover
collect` under `research/inputs/` ([[command:discover-collect]], [[topic:state-layout]]). There is no
research cache: the dated reports under `research/` are what a researcher reads first.
