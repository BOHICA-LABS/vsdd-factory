## Delivers

The work-claim record and its schema; the scopes with what each covers; taking a claim in `vsdd survey`,
in the two admission commands and in `vsdd feature route`; removing it in `vsdd checkpoint` and in a
full sync; `vsdd claim take`; the refusal of a tracked write to a unit this clone does not hold;
withholding at sync; claiming what is in flight when team use is turned on or a clone joins. The
survey's function for the batch of child stories a run will start, with its test against
`runStories()`. The checks of the table work-claim-checks that name no other package. The `elsewhere`
field of the survey object and the `work_claims` field of the status object. One failing and one
passing case per refusal. Takes ledger entries D33-5, D33-6 and D33-32, the `claim take` half of D33-4,
the claim lines of D33-19, and the lease half of P3OPS-13
