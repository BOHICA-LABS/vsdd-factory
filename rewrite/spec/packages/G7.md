## Implements

- [[command:gate-contracts]]: The multi-repository contracts gate, run when a provider's published interface changed since its consumers were last built.
- [[command:gate-ingest]]: Measures ingest coverage and the baseline record of an existing codebase.
- [[command:gate-integration]]: The multi-repository integration gate: brings the services up together and runs cross-repository end-to-end tests.
- [[command:gate-sweep]]: Runs every measured maintenance sweep that is due as one job.
- [[command:release]]: One idempotent command that prepares and then publishes a release; the only route onto the release branch.
- [[command:repos-split]]: Creates member repositories after a spec decision and appends their entries to factory.toml.
- [[record:baseline]]: The closure record of an ingest or an import, holding what was measured then; gates fail only on what is new against it.
