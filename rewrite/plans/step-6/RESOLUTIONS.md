is asked of the orchestrating session are in `ORDER.md`. One line per finding: its id, the outcome
(fixed, rejected or needs-a-person) and what was done. Where a plan and the review differ, the plan
holds. "W2" is `story-workflow.md`, "FR" is `first-run.md`. No plan of steps 3 to 5 was edited: what a
step 6 plan needs from one of them is a row of `ORDER.md` section 8.1.
