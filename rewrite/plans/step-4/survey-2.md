# Step 4 plan: story positions, measuring a gate again, and the checkpoint mapping (S3, second part)

Spec files are under `rewrite/spec/`, code under `next/`. `P/` is `plugins/vsdd-factory/`, `ST/` is
`P/py/vsdd/state/`. "survey.md", "records.md", "state-core.md" and "jobs.md" are the plans in
`rewrite/plans/step-3/`; a decision of survey.md is written `1-D<n>`. `ORDER.md` is this directory's.
`<s>` is a story id.

## 1. Scope

This unit finishes what one `story` run needs from S3 (`packages/S3.md`; `packaging/build-plan.md`, "S3 is
built in two parts"):

1. the story's position as one ladder over evidence and the tool's verdict;
2. the survey measuring a gate again through the gate registry, with a pending answer, when the closure
   names the gate as passed at the worktree's head and no evidence covers that head;
3. the checkpoint mapping step records, evidence, verdict and delivery state to stage entries with their
   own commits, the closure's verdict and `flaky`, and at most one decision per story, with its packet;
4. the next survey reading those decisions: a waiting story is refused, and `waived`, `guidance`,
   `blocked` and `rounds_used` are filled;
5. three paths in the story unit that the step 5 methods need.

Left for when the build widens:

- Restoring red evidence at an earlier commit (a clone without the local evidence, mid-story): refused out
  loud (D3) until G2 can measure red at a recorded commit. After a rebase: G4a (VSA-01).
- Descoping: refused out loud (D6); S3 with the wave survey, G4b. `spec-gap amend`, `open-spike`: S3, S6.
- The unit's `deferred` and `open_pr`, adoption, the `conflict` unit, `spikes_used`: W3, G4b, S6.
- The `merge` decision and the status `needs_human` (G4b): local delivery never writes `awaiting_merge`
  (delivery.md D9; PL4-12), so a governed story goes `stuck`. Sending a notification (S3 when widened;
  PL4-18): the default channel is `none`, and one that is set is logged, not sent (3.2).
- Holdout evaluations, walled findings, retiring research, spike worktrees, closing a cycle, the other
  workflows, diff patches written by the survey (the green gate writes the patch).

## 3. Interfaces this unit provides

### 3.1 `vsdd survey story` (spec `commands/survey.md`; what is not said here is survey.md 3.1)

Order: usage; marker; preconditions; **the decision check**; flush; `run_begin`; admission; **the
re-measure**; the unit; print.

- The decision check reads `unit_decisions(...)["blocking"]` (3.3). Status `open`: `error` "story `<s>`
  waits on decision `<id>`", with the `decide` line and the packet path. Status `rejected`: "story `<s>`
  was descoped by decision `<id>`; not built yet". Both: exit 1, `ok: false`, nothing written, no lock.
- The re-measure is `remeasure.settle` (3.4) with `wait = jobs.clamp_wait(inv.wait)` and
  `retry = inv.retry_command()`. `pending` prints exactly `{"ok": true, "state": "pending", "retry":
  <retry>}` (checked against `pending-output.schema.json`, not the survey schema) and exits 0; run and
  lock are kept, the clerk runs the command again and records' `run_begin` returns the same run. `failed` or
  `unrestorable` releases the lock, which ends the run: exit 1, `ok: false`, the gate's error, `run_id`
  and `units` empty.
- New unit fields: `bc_paths` (the absolute path of each id in `bcs`, same order, by S1's `path_for`),
  `conventions` (the absolute path of `conventions.md`; empty when the project has none, like
  `review_rules`), `demo_dir` (absolute `.factory/demo/<cycle>/<s>`; not created).

### 3.2 `vsdd checkpoint story` (spec `commands/checkpoint.md`; what is not said here is survey.md 3.2)

For each story of the run whose closure is not `closed`: `a = unit_outcome.assess(...)`; a decision to
open goes to `packets.open_decision`; one `closure_update` then sets `position`, the stage entries of `a`
(only when new or when the `sha` changed, so `at` says when that commit reached the stage), `verdict`
when `a` has one, `flaky` (joined with the stored list), `decision` (D4), and for a delivered story
`state: closed` and `closed`, after which its evidence is dropped (`evidence_drop`;
`records/evidence.md`). Nothing is sent: with a decision opened and `notify.channel` not `none`, the
event `notify.failed` (`not built`) is logged.

- `status`, first match: `failed` with `--outcome failed`; `done` when every story of the run is
  delivered; `blocked` when an undelivered story's closure names an open decision; else `partial`.
- `decisions`: `{id, kind, summary}` for each decision this call opened. `progress`: the first part's
  rule, or `a["progress"]`, or a decision opened. `next`: the route's (row 15 gives `human` with the ids).

### 3.3 `vsdd.state.story_unit` (spec `topics/story-life.md`)

- `STAGES`: (position, stage entry, gate) rows: `red`/`tests`/`red`, `green`/`implement`/`green`,
  `reviewed`/`review`/`review`, `evidenced`/`evidence`/`demo`, `delivered`/`deliver`/none. A story's stage
  names are written nowhere else.
- `ladder(root, cycle, story) -> list[dict]`: one rung per row,
  `{position, stage, gate, needed, holds, sha, record}`. `record` is `evidence(root, gate, story)`, which
  for `review` is G3's `covering`; `sha` is its `sha` (`head` for `review`). A rung is not needed, and
  holds, for `red` when the kind has no red gate and for `evidenced` when the story's `demo` is false.
  With `delivery.merged_sha` set every rung holds and no evidence is read. Raises what `evidence` raises.
- `position(root, cycle, story) -> str`: `delivered`; else the position of the last rung before the first
  that does not hold; else `ready`.
- `tool_verdict(root, story) -> dict`: `vsdd.state.verdict(root, story)` (G3, section 4), always an
  object, reached nowhere else in this unit. A missing function raises.
- `unit_decisions(root, cycle, story) -> dict`: `{blocking, guidance, owed_pass}`. The unit's decisions
  are the cycle's `spec-gap.<s>`, `blocked.<s>` and `finding.<s>`. `blocking` is
  `{id, kind, status, reason, note, settled}` of the one the closure's `decision` names, else of the first
  that is `open`, else `None`. When it is `revise`, `guidance` is its `note` and `owed_pass` is true.
- In `build`: `waived` is G3's `verdicts.waivers(root, story)` and `rounds_used` is G3's
  `verdicts.rounds_used(root, story)`, with `owed_pass` at most `convergence.passes - 1`, because
  `direct` grants exactly one more round. This unit builds no waiver list, pass count or closure form
  of its own (PL4-5). `blocked` is `{kind, decision: id, open}` of `blocking`, else empty.

### 3.4 `vsdd.state.remeasure`

`settle(root, *, cycle, story, run, wait, retry) -> dict`: `{state, gate, error}`, `state` one of `ok`,
`pending`, `failed`, `unrestorable`. It raises only what the stores raise.

1. A delivered story, or a closure with no stage entry: `ok`.
2. Red is needed, `evidence("red")` is `None`, and the closure's `tests` entry is `done` at a commit that
   is an ancestor of the head and not the head: `unrestorable`, with the error of D3.
3. For `red`, `green`, `demo` in order, where the rung is needed. Evidence present: next gate. The
   closure's entry for the stage is not `done` at the worktree's head, or
   `gate_evidence.registered_gate(gate)` (called through the module, so a test can replace it) is
   `None`: stop with `ok`; the position says what is left. Otherwise call
   `fn(root, story, run, wait=<whole seconds of wait left, 0 at least>, retry=retry)` (gates.md 3.4).
   The function waits inside its own job attach, so the survey polls nothing and `--wait 0` answers at
   once (PL4-8). `state == "pending"` is `pending`; `ok` false is `failed`, with its `error`; `passed`
   false stops with `ok` (the stage goes back to an agent); `passed` true, next gate.

### 3.5 `vsdd.state.unit_outcome` (spec `topics/human-stops.md`, "Which decision a blocked step becomes")

`assess(root, *, run, cycle, story, now=None) -> dict`:
`{position, stages, verdict, flaky, decision, pointer, progress, failed_runs}`.

- `stages`. Undelivered: `{stage: {state: "done", sha, artifacts}}` for each needed rung that holds;
  `artifacts` are those of the run's `done` step records carrying a passing entry of the rung's gate at
  that `sha` (a re-freeze carries `gate: "red"`), else the stored entry's. Delivered: `deliver` at
  `merged_sha`, and `review` at the verdict's head when the tool verdict is converged; the worktree is
  gone, so nothing that would need evidence is written, and stored entries are kept.
- `verdict`: G3's `verdicts.summary(root, story)`, the closure form, of any outcome. It is `None` when
  no pass is stored, and the stored verdict then stays: a fresh clone must not replace a converged
  closure verdict with `failed` and 0 passes (PL4-4).
- `flaky`: that of the green record covering the head and of each passing `green` entry in the run's
  step records.
- `decision`: `None`, or `{kind, id, reason, summary, detail, open}` from the first row that matches. A
  "final record" is, per `step.name`, the run's record with the highest `step.attempt`; no name is parsed.
  1. Delivered: none.
  2. A final record is `blocked`: kind `spec_gap` gives `spec-gap.<s>`; `reviewer_blocked` gives
     `blocked.<s>`, reason `reviewer`; `environment`, reason `environment`. A final record with status
     `needs_spike` gives `blocked.<s>`, reason `spike_limit`.
  3. The tool verdict's outcome is `blocked`: reason `spec_gap` gives `spec-gap.<s>`; `reviewer_blocked`
     gives `blocked.<s>`, reason `reviewer`; `cap` gives `finding.<s>`. Skipped while the unit holds a
     decision settled `revise` and the run stored no record carrying a `pass` (D4).
  4. `failed_runs` has reached `limits.failed_runs_before_stuck`: `blocked.<s>`, reason `stuck`.
  5. None: kinds `gate`, `conflict`, `other`, a `failed` record, and any delivery state but `merged`
     open nothing, and the next run retries.
- `progress`: an entry of `stages` is new or has a new `sha`. `pointer`: the closure's next `decision`
  (D4). `failed_runs`: 0 with progress or a decision from row 2 or 3, else the function below.

`failed_runs(root, cycle, story, run, now) -> int`: over the runs whose `stories` hold the story and which
began after the newest `settled` time of the unit's decisions, newest first, this run included: the
number before the first in whose time (`start` to `ended`, or `now`) a stored stage entry has its `at`.

### 3.6 `vsdd.state.packets` (spec `records/decision.md`)

- `render(kind, story, detail) -> tuple[str, str]`: a summary line (at most 160 characters) and the
  packet: what is decided, the evidence, the kind's choice lines from records' `KINDS`, the `decide` line.
  Evidence: `finding`, the blocking findings (from G3's `public_verdict`), uncovered items and keys
  waived before (D7); `spec-gap`, the findings or the step's reason; `blocked`, the reason's text, and
  for `stuck` the position, the failing gate lines, and the closure's last `delivery.state` with its
  `detail` and, when the state is `refused`, its `needs_person`. Lists are cut ("and N more") to stay
  under 11,000 bytes.
- `open_decision(root, *, cycle, story, decision, run) -> dict`: records' `decision_open` with the packet,
  and the event `decision.opened`. Raises what `decision_open` raises.

## 5. Design decisions

- D1. "Missing or stale" is one test: the closure's entry says `done` at the worktree's head and
  `evidence()` returns nothing (`topics/evidence-lookup.md`). `review` is computed, never measured.
- D2. The survey calls the registered function in its own process and hands it what is left of
  `--wait`: a job of its own around a function that attaches would hold two job slots. The pending
  answer is the gates' own object, which the clerk's prompt handles, so the survey schema gains no field.
- D3. Red can be measured only where the tests fail, and the registered function measures the head. When
  the closure says tests were red at an earlier commit still on the branch and the record is gone, the
  survey stops: "red evidence for `<s>` at `<sha12>` is missing and can be measured only at that commit,
  which is not built yet". Falling back to `ready` would send a test-writer into finished code.
- D4. One decision per story and checkpoint, by the row order of 3.5; the closure's `decision` points at
  it and means "not yet acted on". The checkpoint sets it to the decision it opened or kept, leaves it
  when the run stored no step record for the story, and clears it otherwise, so a person's `direct`
  survives a run that died early.
- D5. Step records carry no time, and results the script makes itself (`gate`, `agent_budget`,
  `spike_limit`, "no usable result") are never stored. So row 2 reads only kinds an agent sets, and
  failure is read from progress: a run with no new stage entry and no decision fails the unit, and two in
  a row open `stuck`, also for a run that only spends its budget. A clone without run records starts
  the count again.
- D6. A story with an open decision waits (`topics/human-stops.md`): the survey refuses it before the
  lock, as `error`, not as a seventh row of `tables/preconditions.toml`. A rejected one is refused too:
  running a descoped story would be wrong, and closing it belongs with the wave.
- D7. Opening `finding.<s>` again replaces the record and the keys waived in it; the packet lists them
  ("Waived before: repeat them to keep them"). Keeping them unasked needs a list in the closure's verdict,
  which its schema types as a count.
- D8. S5P-N1, S5P-N11: the tool owns paths, so the unit carries them; reference and recording paths stay
  in the story file (step 5, PL5-15). The step 5 plan needs no change: its D14 is met once W2 puts
  `u.demo_dir` in the evidence prompt, and its M1 Glob is a fallback once W2 puts `u.bc_paths` in `where`.

## 8. Order of work, and what must exist first

Before the builder starts: every step 3 unit, `gates`, `verdicts` and `rules` have reported (`ORDER.md`
section 2). The rules unit's tests run the survey this unit rewrites and must still pass when it ends.
G4a need not exist: the tests write the delivery fields themselves. No unit runs beside this one.

1. The schema keys, the fixtures, the harness literal and the three paths in `build`, as one step; then
   `tools/build.py` and every check.
2. `story_progress.py`; `STAGES`, `ladder`, `tool_verdict`, `position`; the ladder and first-part tests.
3. `remeasure.py` and the survey's re-measure step.
4. `unit_decisions`, the survey's decision check, the unit fields.
5. `unit_outcome.py`, then `packets.py`.
6. `run_checkpoint`; the launcher round trip; every check and the whole pytest suite.
