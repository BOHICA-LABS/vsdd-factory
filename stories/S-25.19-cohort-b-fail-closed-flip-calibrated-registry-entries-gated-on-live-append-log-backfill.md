# S-25.19: Cohort B Fail-Closed Flip - Calibrated Registry Entries for validate-burst-log (Edit/Write/MultiEdit Arm), regression-gate and convergence-tracker, Gated on the Live Append-Log Backfill

## Narrative

- **As a** factory operator whose three Cohort B validators (`validate-burst-log`'s Edit/Write/MultiEdit arm, `regression-gate`, `convergence-tracker`) currently fail OPEN on fuel exhaustion
- **I want** those exactly three registry entries flipped to `failure_policy = "fail-closed"` only AFTER the shard-cap gate is live, the pre-existing oversized append-logs have been split by the live backfill, and per-validator calibration evidence shows each validator fits under `PRACTICAL_FUEL_CEILING` against the largest post-split shard
- **So that** an INDETERMINATE outcome on these validators gains the same durable-marker and next-advance-gate protection the Cohort A validators already have, without re-triggering the self-inflicted INDETERMINATE loop that Layer 2 exists to eliminate
