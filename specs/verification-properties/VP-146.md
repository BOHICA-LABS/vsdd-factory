# VP-146: Governed Mechanism-A Migration Crash-Recovery Decision Core — Six Kani Harnesses (B2 obl1_kani_proofs Mirror)

## Property Statement

The pure, zero-I/O crash-recovery decision core of the governed mechanism-A migration
(`backfill-append-logs`) — `decide_append_log_recovery`, the modeled txn-record state machine,
the modeled admission predicate, and the abstract two-namespace (`live`/`durable`) `Fs` crash
model — is proven correct by bounded model checking (Kani/CBMC), not sampled. This VP is the
mechanism-A structural sibling of the B2 (BC-1.18.011) OBL-1 Kani suite
(`obl1_kani_proofs.rs`, ADR-052 §Decision 12): the same seven-property program, retyped onto
mechanism-A's `AppendLogIntentLogRecord` / `AppendLogMigrationTxnState` /
`AppendLogIntentLogRecoveryDecision` domain (v1.5: that per-target decision type is replaced by the shared `decide_recovery` / `RecoveryDecision` of ADR-054 Decision 4, delivered by S-25.11, when S-25.06 rebases onto S-25.12), with the B2 suite's two state-machine harnesses
(inductive step + bounded sequence) retained as a pair under obligation a3. It discharges, for
BC-1.18.013's four-file unit, the crash-safety guarantee that VP-143's real-filesystem
fault-injection harness exercises empirically — the two are complementary, not redundant (Kani
proves the finite/sequential/pure core exhaustively; fault-injection confirms the real OS
satisfies the abstract `Fs` model's axioms — ADR-052 §Decision 12 "two-pronged verification
split").

Six obligations (a1..a6), realized as TEN `#[kani::proof]` functions after the v1.1 amendment
(a3 carries two: a single-step inductive harness and a bounded-sequence harness; a1, a4 and a6
each gain one harness for the S-25.06 D1/D2/D3 obligations — the seven pre-existing functions
keep their names, the three new ones are `proof_obl_a1_terminal_reconcile_totality`,
`proof_obl_a4_reservation_quiescence_and_selfheal`, `proof_obl_a6_terminal_reconcile_idempotence`).
The CI job (`kani-mechanism-a`, `--harness proof_obl_a`, `EXPECTED_PROOFS` — 7 before v1.1, 10
after; the constant MUST be changed in the same commit as the harness additions) counts proof
functions, not obligations:

**Defensible claim form (the property statement VP-146 asserts, verbatim from the harness
module doc):** *under abstract filesystem model M, every modeled op/crash sequence of length <= N
keeps the four-file CURRENT.json pointer old-or-new (never torn), and content-verified per-move
recovery never mistakes a merely-existing canonical path for a completed move.*

**Scope boundary (what this VP does NOT claim).** Kani has no semantics for `rename(2)`,

decision logic and of an explicit abstract filesystem model; the real-OS refinement of that
model's axioms (two-namespace live/durable; atomic rename) is VP-143's empirical fault-injection
obligation, not VP-146's. Per-file content-preservation/census correctness is VP-144's.

verification set exactly as B2's fault-injection suite + `obl1_kani_proofs` do under ADR-052
§Decision 12.

All harnesses are `#[cfg(kani)]`-gated (`cfg(kani)` is allowlisted in the workspace
`[workspace.lints.rust.unexpected_cfgs]` block), so a normal `cargo build`/`test`/`clippy` never
compiles them; they run only under `cargo kani` in the `kani` CI job (`.github/workflows/kani.yml`,
whose `kani-mechanism-a` job runs `cargo kani -p factory-dispatcher --harness proof_obl_a` with
an `EXPECTED_PROOFS` anti-silent-partial-verification guard (7 before v1.1; 10 once the three v1.1 harnesses land — the constant is changed in the same commit), alongside the B2 `proof_obl1`
job). Inputs follow the crate's String-field convention (empty path/id fields; single-character
hash constants `"P"`/`"R"`/`"F"`), which is sound and complete for `decide_append_log_recovery`
(v1.5: and for its shared replacement `decide_recovery`, whose added `done_present` input is a plain
boolean) because its only comparisons are `canonical_hash`/`staging_hash` against
`expected_post_hash`/`expected_pre_state`.

### Why kani-proof (and why a separate VP from VP-143)

Recovery totality, safety, and idempotence are universally-quantified properties over a finite
decision domain — exactly Kani's strength — and a crash-trace over every interleaving of a
bounded op sequence is not enumerable by a hand-written fault-injection suite. VP-143 remains
the single-method (integration) real-filesystem VP; folding a kani-proof leg into it would
violate the single-method-per-VP convention that VP-133/VP-124 and BC-1.18.013's own §VP Anchors
establish. A distinct VP also gives the Kani suite its own lifecycle (`verification_lock`,
`proof_completed_date`, `proof_file_hash`) at F6 formal hardening.
